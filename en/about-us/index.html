About Austar

Austar Education and Migration is a registered Australian study and migration agency, incorporated in Queensland in 2015. Our team includes registered migration agents, qualified education agent counsellors and NAATI-certified translators, and we have handled many applications that succeeded after an earlier refusal.

How Austar is different

Study and migration, one team

Qualified education counsellors and registered migration agents work together, so a child’s schooling, a parent’s guardian visa and the visa after graduation can be planned as one, without passing messages between agencies.

EvidenceCase: Sydney private school place + mother’s 590 guardian visa →

On the ground, including Tasmania

We have offices in Brisbane and Hobart, so you can meet your adviser in person. Hobart is a designated regional area; we have handled Tasmanian nominations and know the regional visa requirements.

EvidenceCase: Tasmanian nomination, 491 granted →

A refusal isn’t always the end

A visa or nomination refusal doesn’t always close the door. We work out why it was refused, then look for another realistic pathway.

EvidenceCase: Tasmanian 190 refused → NSW 190 granted →

Still here after the grant

We track students’ visa and enrolment status, and many parents appoint our counsellor as their child’s emergency contact in Australia. 188 to 888, school enrolment and renting: we keep helping.

How we work

  1. Assessment

    We look at your education, work, family and timing to find realistic visa or study pathways.

  2. Plan and quote

    You receive a written plan and itemised fees. Work starts only after the services agreement is signed.

  3. Prepare and lodge

    We gather evidence, write submissions and lodge on schedule, keeping you updated throughout.

  4. After the grant

    Renewals, permanent residence, school enrolment, renting and local emergency contact: we stay with you.
