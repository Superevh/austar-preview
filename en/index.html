Frequently asked questions

Is Austar a registered agency?

Yes. Austar’s migration work is carried out by registered migration agents listed with the Office of the Migration Agents Registration Authority (OMARA), including MARNs 1685007, 1681398, 2014553 and 1798376, which can be checked on the OMARA register. Our education counsellors hold QEAC certification (K469, N279). The company is Easygo Australia Migration & Education Pty Ltd, ACN 606 670 115, registered in Queensland in 2015.

Where are Austar’s offices?

Austar has offices in Brisbane (3 Vermeer Close, MacKenzie QLD 4156, +61 416 295 737) and Hobart (Level 1, 45 Murray St, Hobart TAS 7000, +61 450 826 827), and also works remotely with clients in China.

Which visas does Austar handle?

Skilled visas (189, 190, 491/191), employer sponsored (482), partner visas (820/801, 309/100), contributory parent (143), student (500) and student guardian (590) visas, and 888 permanent residence for existing 188 holders.

Can I apply again after a refusal?

It depends on the reason for refusal and the visa. Austar has helped clients succeed after refusals, for example by securing a different state’s nomination after one state refused. Bring your refusal letter to an assessment.

How do I check whether a migration agent is registered?

Search the agent’s name or MARN on the OMARA “Search the register of migration agents” page. Only registered migration agents and practising lawyers may lawfully give immigration assistance.
